# SRM COLLEGE RULES, REGULATIONS AND STUDENT POLICIES

## 1. INTRODUCTION AND GENERAL STUDENT CONDUCT

SRM Institute of Science and Technology expects every student to maintain high standards of academic discipline, personal conduct, responsibility and respect for the institution. Students are required to understand and follow the regulations applicable to their programme, department and campus.

### 1.1 General Conduct

1. Every student shall comply with the rules, regulations, circulars and instructions issued by the University, college, department and authorised faculty members.

2. Students shall maintain respectful behaviour towards faculty members, staff, visitors and fellow students.

3. Students shall carry their valid college/student identity card whenever required and produce it when requested by authorised personnel.

4. Students shall maintain the cleanliness and appearance of classrooms, laboratories, corridors, libraries and other institutional facilities.

5. Students shall not damage, misuse, remove or interfere with college property, equipment, furniture, computers, laboratory instruments or other institutional resources.

6. Students shall be punctual for lectures, practical classes, examinations, meetings, seminars and other academic activities.

7. Students are responsible for regularly checking official notices, academic announcements and departmental communications.

8. Students shall maintain appropriate academic and professional behaviour both inside and outside the classroom when representing the institution.

9. Students shall follow instructions issued by faculty members, laboratory personnel, examination authorities, security personnel and other authorised officials.

10. Any violation of University rules may result in appropriate disciplinary action according to the applicable regulations.

---

# 2. ATTENDANCE AND ACADEMIC DISCIPLINE

Attendance is an important academic requirement. SRMIST regulations state that students are expected to attend classes, laboratories and other assigned learning activities regularly. The Engineering and Technology handbook specifies a **minimum 75% attendance requirement in individual courses**, subject to applicable University provisions and permitted exemptions. Other SRM programmes may prescribe different requirements; for example, an SRM Pharmacy academic document specifies 80%. Students should therefore follow the requirement applicable to their programme.

### 2.1 Attendance Rules

1. Students shall attend all scheduled lectures, tutorials, laboratory sessions, field work and other compulsory academic activities.

2. Attendance shall be recorded by the concerned faculty member.

3. Students must satisfy the minimum attendance requirement prescribed for their programme and course.

4. Students who fail to meet the prescribed attendance requirement may become ineligible to appear for the relevant end-semester examination, subject to applicable University rules.

5. Students shall not attempt to manipulate, falsify or interfere with attendance records.

6. Students should report genuine attendance-related discrepancies to the concerned faculty member or academic authority promptly.

7. Medical leave, on-duty activities, approved extracurricular activities and other permitted absences shall be handled according to the applicable University regulations.

8. Approval of leave does not automatically guarantee exemption from academic requirements unless specifically permitted by the applicable rules.

9. Students should obtain prior permission wherever required for planned absence.

10. Repeated absence without valid reason may affect academic progress and eligibility for examinations.

### 2.2 Academic Responsibility

1. Students shall complete assignments, records, projects and other academic work within the deadlines specified by the faculty.

2. Students are responsible for understanding course requirements, assessment schedules and submission procedures.

3. Failure to submit academic work within the specified period may result in consequences determined by the course faculty or department.

4. Students shall maintain academic integrity and shall not submit another person's work as their own.

5. Plagiarism, fabrication, falsification and other forms of academic misconduct shall be dealt with according to applicable institutional procedures.

---

# 3. CLASSROOM RULES

The classroom is an academic environment intended to support effective teaching and learning. Students are expected to maintain discipline and avoid behaviour that disturbs other students or faculty.

### 3.1 Before Class

1. Students should arrive before the scheduled beginning of the class.

2. Students should occupy the seats or locations assigned by the faculty when applicable.

3. Students should carry the required textbooks, notebooks, laboratory records, calculators or other permitted academic materials.

4. Students should keep mobile phones and electronic devices in an appropriate mode according to faculty instructions.

### 3.2 During Class

1. Students shall maintain silence when the faculty member is teaching or when another student is presenting.

2. Students shall not engage in unnecessary conversations, shouting, disruptive behaviour or activities unrelated to the class.

3. Students should participate constructively in discussions, activities and learning exercises.

4. Students should raise questions respectfully and at appropriate times.

5. Students shall not leave the classroom repeatedly without valid reason or permission where permission is required.

6. Students shall not sleep, play games or engage in activities that interfere with the learning environment.

7. Students shall not record audio, video or photographs of classes without appropriate permission.

8. Students shall not misuse classroom computers, projectors, smart boards or other teaching equipment.

9. Students shall keep desks, chairs and classroom facilities clean.

10. Food and beverages shall be handled according to the rules of the particular classroom or building.

### 3.3 Classroom Technology

1. Electronic devices may be used for academic purposes when permitted by the faculty.

2. Devices shall not be used to disturb other students.

3. Students shall not use unauthorised recording, communication or examination-related applications during assessments.

4. Students shall comply with instructions regarding laptops, tablets, mobile phones and other devices during classes and examinations.

5. Unauthorised access to college computer systems, accounts or networks is prohibited.

---

# 4. EXAMINATION RULES

Examinations shall be conducted according to the rules prescribed by the Controller of Examinations and the applicable programme regulations. SRMIST's examination policy includes mechanisms relating to evaluation, review, revaluation and retotalling, with procedures differing according to programme requirements.

## 4.1 Before the Examination

1. Students shall verify the examination timetable, venue, date and reporting requirements before the examination.

2. Students should arrive at the examination venue sufficiently early.

3. Students shall carry the required identification and examination materials.

4. Students shall follow instructions issued by examination officials.

5. Students shall sit only in the seat allotted to them where seating arrangements are prescribed.

6. Students shall not bring unauthorised materials into the examination hall.

7. Mobile phones and other electronic devices shall be handled according to the examination instructions.

## 4.2 During the Examination

1. Students shall maintain silence inside the examination hall.

2. Students shall not communicate with other candidates unless specifically permitted.

3. Students shall not exchange papers, stationery, calculators or other materials without permission.

4. Students shall use only permitted writing instruments and examination materials.

5. Students shall not use unauthorised notes, books, electronic devices, communication devices or other prohibited materials.

6. Students shall follow all instructions given by the invigilator.

7. Students shall not attempt to obtain or provide unauthorised assistance.

8. Students shall not copy another student's answers or allow another student to copy their work.

9. Students shall not disturb other candidates during the examination.

10. Students shall remain in the examination venue according to the rules and timings applicable to that examination.

11. Students shall submit their answer scripts and other required materials as instructed.

12. Any suspected malpractice or examination misconduct may be reported to the appropriate examination authority.

## 4.3 Examination Malpractice

Examination malpractice includes, but is not limited to:

* Possession or use of unauthorised study materials.
* Unauthorised communication with another candidate.
* Use of prohibited electronic devices.
* Copying or attempting to copy.
* Providing assistance to another candidate.
* Substitution or impersonation.
* Tampering with examination documents.
* Obtaining unauthorised access to examination information.
* Any other action intended to obtain an unfair academic advantage.

Cases of malpractice shall be handled under the applicable examination rules and disciplinary procedures.

## 4.4 Revaluation and Grievance

Students who believe that an examination result requires review may use the applicable review, revaluation or retotalling procedure.

SRMIST's published examination policy states that the available review mechanisms can vary by programme and may include review, revaluation and/or retotalling. It also states that review/revaluation/retotalling is not permitted for practical, clinical or viva voce examinations under that policy.

Students must submit applications within the prescribed time and follow the procedure announced by the examination authority.

# 5. LABORATORY RULES AND SAFETY

Laboratories require strict discipline because students work with equipment, instruments, electrical systems, chemicals or other potentially hazardous materials. SRMIST laboratory guidance specifically emphasises supervision, reporting accidents, maintaining clean workspaces, appropriate protective equipment and safe handling of laboratory materials.

## 5.1 General Laboratory Conduct

1. Students shall enter laboratories only during authorised laboratory sessions or with permission.

2. Students shall follow the instructions of the faculty member, laboratory instructor, technician or laboratory in-charge.

3. Students shall not run, push, engage in practical jokes or behave in a manner that creates a safety risk.

4. Students shall not sit on laboratory benches unless the laboratory specifically provides seating for that purpose.

5. Bags and personal belongings shall be kept in designated areas.

6. Students shall keep laboratory benches and working areas clean and organised.

7. Students shall not remove equipment, chemicals, samples or other laboratory materials without permission.

8. Students shall not operate equipment without appropriate instruction or authorisation.

9. Students shall not perform experiments without appropriate supervision when supervision is required.

10. Students shall report equipment damage, spills, accidents, injuries or unsafe conditions immediately.

## 5.2 Laboratory Equipment

1. Students shall inspect equipment before use where required.

2. Equipment shall be operated only according to the instructions provided.

3. Students shall not modify laboratory equipment without permission.

4. Electrical equipment shall be handled safely and carefully.

5. Students shall switch off equipment after use when instructed.

6. Broken or damaged equipment shall not be used.

7. Any malfunction shall be reported to the laboratory staff.

8. Students shall not attempt unauthorised repairs.

## 5.3 Chemical and Material Safety

1. Students shall verify labels before using chemicals or laboratory materials.

2. Chemicals shall be handled only according to the instructions given by the faculty or laboratory manual.

3. Students shall use appropriate protective equipment when required.

4. Chemicals shall not be removed from their designated locations without permission.

5. Waste materials shall be disposed of in the appropriate containers.

6. Broken glass and sharp objects shall be disposed of according to laboratory safety procedures.

7. Students shall not taste, intentionally inhale or otherwise improperly handle laboratory chemicals.

8. Chemical spills shall be reported immediately.

9. Emergency equipment and exits shall remain accessible.

10. Students shall familiarise themselves with the location of first-aid equipment, emergency exits and other safety facilities.

## 5.4 Personal Protective Equipment

Where required, students shall use suitable protective equipment such as:

* Laboratory coat or protective apron.
* Safety goggles.
* Protective gloves.
* Appropriate footwear.
* Other programme-specific protective equipment.

Long hair and loose clothing should be secured when working with laboratory equipment or materials. SRMIST laboratory guidance specifically recommends protective clothing, appropriate footwear and securing long hair or loose clothing.

# 6. COMPUTER LAB AND DIGITAL RESOURCE RULES

Computer laboratories and digital facilities shall be used primarily for academic, research and authorised institutional purposes.

### 6.1 Computer Usage

1. Students shall use only the computer account or credentials assigned to them.

2. Students shall not share passwords or access another person's account.

3. Students shall not install unauthorised software.

4. Students shall not alter system configurations without permission.

5. Students shall not attempt to bypass security controls.

6. Students shall not access, modify or delete another person's files.

7. Students shall not introduce malware or other harmful software into institutional systems.

8. Students shall use internet access responsibly and in accordance with institutional policies.

9. Students shall not use laboratory computers for activities that disrupt academic work.

10. Students shall report technical problems to the responsible laboratory or IT personnel.

### 6.2 Data and Academic Integrity

Students shall protect academic and personal data and shall not misuse institutional information systems.

Unauthorised copying, distribution or alteration of academic records, examination materials or institutional data is prohibited.

---

# 7. LIBRARY AND CAMPUS FACILITY RULES

The library and other shared facilities are intended for academic use. SRMIST library rules include requirements such as carrying identification, maintaining silence and following procedures for personal belongings and library materials.

### 7.1 Library Conduct

1. Students shall carry their valid identification where required.

2. Silence shall be maintained in designated reading areas.

3. Mobile phones shall be used only in accordance with library rules.

4. Books shall be handled carefully.

5. Students shall not write on, tear, fold or otherwise damage library materials.

6. Books shall be returned within the prescribed period.

7. Students shall follow the library's borrowing and renewal procedures.

8. Personal belongings shall be stored in designated areas where required.

9. Students shall not disturb other library users.

10. Any applicable fine or replacement procedure shall be followed for lost or damaged materials.

### 7.2 Campus Property

1. Students shall protect college buildings, furniture, equipment and infrastructure.

2. Vandalism and intentional damage to institutional property are prohibited.

3. Students shall use classrooms, seminar halls, sports facilities and other common areas responsibly.

4. Students shall dispose of waste in designated bins.

5. Students shall comply with campus safety and security instructions.

---

# 8. DRESS, IDENTITY AND PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOUR

Students are expected to maintain a neat, appropriate and programme-appropriate appearance consistent with the requirements of their college, department, laboratory or professional training environment.

### 8.1 Identity Card

1. Students shall carry their institutional identity card when required.

2. Students shall produce the identity card to authorised officials when requested.

3. Students shall not lend their identity card to another person.

4. Lost identity cards should be reported promptly.

### 8.2 Professional Behaviour

1. Students shall communicate respectfully with faculty, staff and peers.

2. Abusive, threatening or seriously disruptive behaviour is prohibited.

3. Students shall respect the personal space, dignity and rights of other members of the institution.

4. Students shall not engage in harassment, bullying, discrimination or other prohibited conduct.

# 9. DISCIPLINE, RESPONSIBILITIES AND VIOLATIONS

Students are responsible for understanding the consequences of violating institutional rules.

### 9.1 Examples of Disciplinary Violations

The following may constitute violations:

1. Repeated disruption of classes.

2. Deliberate damage to college property.

3. Examination malpractice.

4. Falsification of academic records.

5. Unauthorised access to institutional systems.

6. Theft or unauthorised removal of institutional property.

7. Serious misconduct towards faculty, staff or students.

8. Violation of laboratory safety requirements.

9. Failure to follow legitimate instructions from authorised personnel.

10. Any other conduct prohibited under applicable University regulations.

### 9.2 Disciplinary Procedure

Where a student is alleged to have violated a rule:

1. The matter may be reported to the appropriate authority.

2. The student may be asked to provide an explanation.

3. The institution may conduct an inquiry according to the applicable rules.

4. Appropriate action may be taken based on the findings.

5. Students shall have access to applicable grievance or appeal mechanisms where provided.

6. Disciplinary action shall be determined according to the seriousness and circumstances of the violation and the applicable University regulations.

# 10. STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES AND FINAL POLICY CLAUSES

### 10.1 Core Responsibilities

Every student is expected to:

* Attend classes regularly.
* Maintain the required attendance percentage.
* Arrive on time.
* Complete assignments and academic work.
* Follow examination rules.
* Maintain academic integrity.
* Follow laboratory safety procedures.
* Protect college property.
* Respect faculty, staff and fellow students.
* Follow campus safety requirements.
* Use technology responsibly.
* Maintain cleanliness.
* Read official notices and communications.
* Seek clarification from the appropriate department when uncertain about a rule.

### 10.2 Important Final Clauses

**Clause 1 – Compliance:**
All students shall comply with the rules and regulations applicable to their programme, department and campus.

**Clause 2 – Amendments:**
The institution may amend, modify, introduce or withdraw rules and procedures as required. Students shall comply with the latest officially communicated requirements.

**Clause 3 – Programme-Specific Rules:**
Where programme-specific, professional-body or regulatory requirements differ from general college rules, the applicable programme-specific regulations shall be followed.

**Clause 4 – Examination Authority:**
Examination-related matters shall be governed by the applicable examination regulations and instructions issued by the authorised examination authorities.

**Clause 5 – Attendance:**
Students must satisfy the attendance requirement applicable to their course and programme to remain eligible for examinations and academic progression.

**Clause 6 – Laboratory Safety:**
No student shall undertake laboratory work in a manner that creates an avoidable risk to themselves, other persons, equipment or facilities.

**Clause 7 – Academic Integrity:**
Students shall perform academic work honestly and shall not obtain an unfair academic advantage through cheating, plagiarism, fabrication or other misconduct.

**Clause 8 – Institutional Property:**
Students shall use institutional property responsibly and may be held accountable for intentional or negligent damage in accordance with applicable procedures.

**Clause 9 – Respect and Discipline:**
Students shall maintain respectful and professional conduct throughout the campus and during activities representing the institution.

**Clause 10 – Responsibility to Stay Informed:**
Students are responsible for reading official notices, regulations, timetables and instructions issued by the University, college, department and authorised offices.

## CONCLUSION

The purpose of these rules is to maintain a safe, disciplined, inclusive and academically productive college environment. Compliance with academic, examination, classroom, laboratory, technology and campus regulations is an essential responsibility of every student.

These rules should be read together with the latest official SRMIST regulations, programme-specific regulations, examination instructions, departmental guidelines and other policies issued by the institution. The applicable official regulation should always take precedence where a programme-specific rule differs from a general rule.
